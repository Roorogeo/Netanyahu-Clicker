Legacy Tree

Every Knesset seat you win in an election gives 1 Political Capital. Spend it on permanent perks that survive every election. Start at the bottom and work up; the crown at the top needs all four branches.

Endless Mandates

Repeatable perks for Political Capital. Each level costs more than the last; some never stop.

Eras

Fill the Knesset (120 seats) to begin a new Era. Heritage comes from your career vote total, so each new Era needs more progress than the last. Each of your first 30 Heritage points adds +10% production forever, and more Heritage keeps adding at a slower rate. Beginning an Era resets seats, Ministers, Political Capital and Endless Mandates. Your Legacy Tree is kept.
